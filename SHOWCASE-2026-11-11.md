# CJIT Metrocoms 911 — November 11, 2026 showcase update

**Release type:** public-facing product update; not a source-code release.

## What's new in the hosted application
- Unified CJIT IAM authentication gate for assigned operators and administrators.
- Role-scoped access to Metrocoms console functions.
- Existing simulation, radio, AI, and administrative service bindings preserved during the IAM migration.

**Verification:** Cloudflare Worker deployment and IAM service binding were confirmed on November 11, 2026. End-to-end sign-in, incoming call audio, radio push-to-talk, voice fallback, and CAD synchronization still require live tests.

## Demo availability
A restricted demonstration environment is planned with fictional incidents, synthetic caller identities, sample dispatch units and resettable training sessions. Demonstration accounts must not access production records, real emergency services, internal service credentials, or proprietary server-side source.

**Product:** https://911.cjitnetworks.com (authentication required)
